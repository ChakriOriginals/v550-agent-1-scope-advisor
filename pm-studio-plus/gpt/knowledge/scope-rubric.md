# V550 Stage 1 Advisory Scope Rubric

The Auto-Grader/Evaluator scores eight equally weighted dimensions from 1 to 5. Scores are feedback and report evidence only: they cannot open or close a gate, alter a hard-check result, or set a Canvas grade.

## Common scale

- **1:** absent, materially incorrect, or unsupported;
- **2:** partial, vague, inconsistent, or heavily AI-dependent;
- **3:** competent and complete enough to proceed, with identifiable weaknesses;
- **4:** strong, coherent, well-justified, and course-authentic;
- **5:** exceptionally precise, integrated, evidence-aware, and defensible without being overbuilt.

## Dimension anchors

1. **Project Statement**
   - **3:** trigger, action, timing/frequency, scope, and assumptions/constraints/uncertainty are present and mostly coherent.
   - **5:** all components are precise, mutually coherent, decision-useful, and clearly grounded in the student's project.
2. **Objectives & Success Criteria**
   - **3:** most objectives are SMART and most criteria are measurable and linked.
   - **5:** every objective passes SMART, can be tested as a yes/no accomplishment question, and has a one-to-one tangible criterion.
3. **Scope of Work**
   - **3:** the high-level boundary is understandable with minor gray areas.
   - **5:** in-scope, out-of-scope, and the high-level work are precise, aligned to requirements, and do not drift into detailed scheduling.
4. **Deliverables**
   - **3:** deliverables are mostly outputs, logically ordered, and phased.
   - **5:** every deliverable is an objective-linked output with dependency logic and an explicit approval point.
5. **Scope Action Plan**
   - **3:** most deliverables have action-verb activities and owners.
   - **5:** every deliverable has complete, assignable, justified, milestone-level actions with no Lone-Wolf or ownerless gaps.
6. **Constraints & Uncertainties**
   - **3:** major limitations and unresolved issues are named.
   - **5:** constraints, assumptions, uncertainties, and verification needs are clearly distinguished, prioritized, and connected to consequences.
7. **Exclusions**
   - **3:** major exclusions are explicit.
   - **5:** exclusions are comprehensive enough to prevent foreseeable scope creep and are justified against requirements and expectations.
8. **Do You Deliver?**
   - **3:** the Stage 1 package is coherent and usable after ordinary revision.
   - **5:** the student's package is professionally defensible, internally integrated, realistic, and supported by meaningful critique and justification.

Scores 2 and 4 interpolate between the stated anchors using the common scale. For every dimension, the private evaluator response contains the score, concise rationale, one or more short evidence excerpts, evidence locator, missing evidence, confidence, and the single most useful improvement action. Evidence excerpts stay in the private session and are never sent to telemetry.

Action-line ownership is advisory rubric evidence at Gate 5; it is not an additional Gate 5 hard check. The explicit Gate 6 work-package ownership check remains blocking. Thin revision history, critique depth, AI-reliance metrics, misconception flags, and all rubric scores remain non-blocking signals. Gate status comes only from the applicable hard checks in `generated/frozen-six-gates.md`.
