## Current organization

Eighteen months after the merger completed, Bloomington Playwrights Project, Cardinal Stage, and Pigasus Institute operate in this course scenario as the merged **Meridian Arts Collective**.

Its continuing programs are:

- **New Play Development:** commissions and workshops new scripts and runs a spring festival of staged readings.
- **Mainstage:** runs a five-show subscription season from September through May.
- **Learning & Media:** runs youth classes, adult workshops, and a small documentary-film program.

Meridian has 11 full-time staff and about 30 contract artists per season. The three legacy artistic leads stayed and report to Executive Director Dana Okoye. Dana was hired from outside all three organizations eight months ago. Meetings remain polite, but space allocation is unresolved.

## Planning history

- An outside consultant produced the merger plan for **$90,000 over fourteen months**.
- The **214-page integration plan** completed governance and branding but not facilities or programming. Staff call it **"the binder."**
- A website consolidation later finished **four months late**.
- A donor-database merge was abandoned after **five months**, with no written record explaining why.
- Legacy Mainstage staff say the merger was done **to** them.
- Learning & Media staff say the merger was done **around** them.
- No current staff member has run a formal planning process in-house.

## Building and source material

Meridian leases the John Waldron Arts Center from the City of Bloomington. The building has about **38,000 square feet**:

- **Ruth Auditorium:** 232 fixed seats and a full fly system.
- **Firebay black box:** 87 flexible seats.
- **Main Gallery:** 2,100 square feet and street-facing.
- **Corridor Gallery:** 900 square feet.
- **Studios A–F:** six rooms of 400–800 square feet.
- **Basement scene shop:** 1,800 square feet, shared.
- **Admin suite:** nine desks.
- **Shared access:** one loading dock and one freight elevator.

An unread **2019 space-utilization study** is in the admin-suite filing cabinet. It includes floor plans, room dimensions, and one year of booking data.
