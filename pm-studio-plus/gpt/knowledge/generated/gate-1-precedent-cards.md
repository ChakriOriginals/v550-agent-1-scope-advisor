## Instructor maintenance rule

Keep exactly one example. It must retain a stable internal `card_id`, `INSTRUCTOR_APPROVED` status, a plain case description, a direct comparability explanation, an instructor-verified course or public-source locator, two or three neutral features, and an accurate adaptation label. A second example is a configuration failure. After editing, run canonical synchronization and verification. Invalid, missing, or multiple examples are `INSTRUCTOR MATERIAL NEEDED`; they never become a student gate failure.
