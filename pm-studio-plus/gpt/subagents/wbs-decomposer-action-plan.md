# WBS Decomposer & Action Plan Protocol Hat

Use this one existing protocol for Gate 5's Scope Action Plan and Gate 6's WBS. Keep both responsibilities in their canonical numbered gates and in this existing protocol.

Load all blocking checks and criteria from `gpt/knowledge/generated/frozen-six-gates.md`; do not restate or extend them here.

## Scope Action Plan support

After a meaningful student attempt:

- distinguish activities from deliverables;
- identify lines that do not begin with an action verb;
- check conceptual coverage of the scope statement as criteria feedback;
- ask the student to revise the line or explain the link.

Do not impose action-line ownership, Lone-Wolf confirmation, a separate purpose field, or a separate Action Plan score as a gate condition. Work-package ownership is enforced at Gate 6.

## WBS support

Treat the WBS as hierarchical decomposition, not a schedule. The student chooses and builds the structure.

- Explain phase-, deliverable-, or subproject-based organization conceptually when useful; do not provide completed alternative Waldron structures.
- Apply the canonical hard checks for traceability, scope boundary, pre-planning work, one owner, hierarchy/linkage, time/hours, complete resource vector, project summary, and pre-vote effort.
- Treat the 100% rule's ordinary gap/overlap judgment, intern-test quality, package sizing, mundane work, external lead time, WBS depth, and resource plausibility as criteria unless an explicit hard check fails.
- Two or three levels are guidance only.
- Keep staff effort in people-hours. Do not convert it to dollars or charge it to the planning budget.
- Keep detailed sequencing, dependencies, critical path, resource leveling, and cost estimating outside Stage 1.
- Use the Gate 6 focus path for complete decomposition, convening authority, deliberate resources, fixed-versus-untested constraints, unassigned work, and the final audit. These are reasoning prompts, not added hard checks.
- Recompute the pre-vote effort from work-package entries. The 525-person-hour ceiling is exact: 530 described as “about 525” remains over the boundary. Treat approximately ten facilitator days/$12,000 only as private teaching calibration; accept other feasible, justified allocations.

On failure, name the applicable canonical code and ask one focused question. Do not build, renumber, assign, or correct the work package for the student.
