# V550 Stage 1 Advisory Rubric

Score eight equally weighted dimensions from 1 to 5. This rubric supports feedback, calibration, and the learning report. It cannot open or close a gate, override a deterministic hard check, or change the Canvas LMS grade.

## Common scale

- **1:** absent, materially incorrect, or unsupported;
- **2:** partial, vague, inconsistent, or heavily AI-dependent;
- **3:** competent and complete enough to proceed, with identifiable weaknesses;
- **4:** strong, coherent, well-justified, and course-authentic;
- **5:** exceptionally precise, integrated, evidence-aware, and defensible without being overbuilt.

| Dimension | Score 3 anchor | Score 5 anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Project Statement | Trigger, action, timing/frequency, scope, and assumptions/constraints/uncertainty are present and mostly coherent. | All components are precise, mutually coherent, decision-useful, and clearly grounded in the student's project. |
| Objectives & Success Criteria | Most objectives are SMART and most criteria are measurable and linked. | Every objective passes SMART, can be tested as a yes/no accomplishment question, and has a one-to-one tangible criterion. |
| Scope of Work | The high-level boundary is understandable with minor gray areas. | In-scope, out-of-scope, and the high-level work are precise, aligned to requirements, and do not drift into detailed scheduling. |
| Deliverables | Deliverables are mostly outputs, logically ordered, and phased. | Every deliverable is an objective-linked output with dependency logic and an explicit approval point. |
| Scope Action Plan | Most deliverables have action-verb activities and owners. | Every deliverable has complete, assignable, justified, milestone-level actions with no Lone-Wolf or ownerless gaps. |
| Constraints & Uncertainties | Major limitations and unresolved issues are named. | Constraints, assumptions, uncertainties, and verification needs are clearly distinguished, prioritized, and connected to consequences. |
| Exclusions | Major exclusions are explicit. | Exclusions are comprehensive enough to prevent foreseeable scope creep and are justified against requirements and expectations. |
| Do You Deliver? | The Stage 1 package is coherent and usable after ordinary revision. | The student's package is professionally defensible, internally integrated, realistic, and supported by meaningful critique and justification. |

Scores 2 and 4 interpolate between these anchors using the common scale. Every private evaluator result includes score, concise rationale, short evidence excerpt(s), evidence locator, missing evidence, confidence, and one best improvement action. Telemetry receives scores, evidence counts/locators or reason codes—not excerpts or drafts.

An ownerless Scope Action Plan line may lower the advisory Gate 5 dimension score, but it is not a Gate 5 hard check. Gate 6's explicit one-owner-per-work-package check remains blocking. Thin learning history may be flagged for instructor context but never changes a gate result or dimension score. Only the applicable hard checks from the canonical six-gate contract determine status.
