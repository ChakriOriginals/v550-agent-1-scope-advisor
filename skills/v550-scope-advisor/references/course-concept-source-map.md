## Focus-question families

The exact student-visible subheadings and questions are controlled by `frozen-six-gates.md`, `Focus-question teaching path`. Their calibration sources are:

- Gate 1: C7 slides 24–46 plus the scenario planning history, authority, sponsor, committee, and building sections.
- Gates 2–4: C8 slides 21–60 plus the scenario agreements, lease, stakeholders, authority, and fixed checkpoints.
- Gate 5: C9 slides 21–45 plus Gate 5 exclusions and fixed deliverable deadlines.
- Gate 6: C10 slides 13–33 plus the frozen Gate 6 and Gate 6B contracts.

Focus questions guide reasoning only. They never create a hard check, answer bank, required patron, required facilitator allocation, or hidden evaluator dimension.
